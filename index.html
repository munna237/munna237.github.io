Research Assistant

North South University

Dhaka, Bangladesh

I am a Computer Science and Engineering researcher and current Research Assistant at North South University who enjoys both teaching and discovery. I have 15 peer-reviewed publications in medical imaging, computer vision, and embedded AI, with additional manuscripts in progress alongside collaborators in the United States, Canada, and at Intel. I aim to help students learn to question, build, and explore, while continuing research that makes AI useful and trustworthy in real-world settings, whether as a faculty member or through graduate study.

My research interests include medical image analysis, computer vision, knowledge distillation, IoT & edge AI (TinyML), AI agents & LLM safety, and biomedical & health informatics.
