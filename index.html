About

I'm a Computer Science graduate of East West University, Dhaka (BSc CSE, Feb 2022 – Aug 2026), working at the intersection of deep learning and biomedical imaging.

I work as a Research Intern at AMIR Lab (DAIBI) on lightweight deep learning models for biomedical imaging, and previously as a Research Assistant at CCDS, Independent University Bangladesh, on medical image segmentation with self-supervised learning.

My undergraduate thesis, Self-Supervised Learning for Offline MRI Reconstruction, trains reconstruction models without fully sampled ground truth — the setting that actually holds in hospitals that cannot spare scanner time for reference acquisitions.

I have eleven peer-reviewed papers (first author on seven) and six manuscripts under review at JMRI, PLOS ONE, Smart Agricultural Technology, and IEEE Access.

I review for IEEE Access and IJECE, and for NeurIPS 2026 and the MICCAI 2026 ShapeMI Workshop. I serve as Secretary of the IEEE Computer Society Bangladesh Chapter.
